Evaluations

Two analyses, one teaching record

These two interactive reports were made from overlapping sets of Benjamin Collier's de-identified teaching evaluations. They use different time windows, which is why their headline response counts differ.

02

The denominator, made explicit

727 recent responses + 430 earlier responses = 1,157 total responses

Version A combines all 44 comparable five-point sections from 2011 to 2026. Version B uses the 24 sections since Fall 2023 for its headline and places the earlier CMU-Q record in a separate section. The pages are therefore different views of overlapping evidence, not conflicting counts.

Faculty Twin SMS alerts

Prof Collier AI (Ben Collier). How the instructor opts in to text alerts from his Faculty Twin course assistant.

No. 01

Opt in

Text START to (412) 797-6065 to receive Faculty Twin alerts from Prof Collier AI.

These alerts are for one person only: Ben Collier, the instructor who runs Faculty Twin. When a student reports a technical problem in the course assistant (an API key out of credit, a broken submission or a broken quiz), he gets one short text so he can fix it. Students never receive texts, and no one else's number is used.

After texting START you get: "Faculty Twin alerts are on. Msg frequency varies, up to 10 msgs/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

  • Message frequency varies, up to 10 messages per day.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out at any time.

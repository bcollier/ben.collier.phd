70-377 Managing and Assessing Tech Talent and Organizations

Course I built

70-377

How to hire, develop, and evaluate technical people using evidence instead of instinct.

A micro-course I developed on managing technical talent: how to assess skill, how to design the work, and how to build teams that deliver. I wrote it for undergraduates, and it ran for the first time at CMU Qatar in Fall 2025.

How the course developed

I proposed this micro-course to Carnegie Mellon in Qatar in February 2025 and taught it in Fall 2025. Most sessions met on Zoom in the evening, Doha time, and the middle block met in person in Doha over three days. It was open to technical and business students with no prerequisites.

Topics

  1. Interviewing for software development roles, including AI-driven assessment
  2. Interviewing for data and quantitative roles
  3. Case and behavioral interviews
  4. Onboarding technical talent: the first ninety days
  5. Running technical projects and leading an effective team
  6. Project management with work breakdown structures and OKRs
  7. Managing performance, promotion, and termination

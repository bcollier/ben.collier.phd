70-323 Empirical Research Methods

70-323

Ask a question about people, design a survey, collect the data, and test it with regression.

About 23 students

This course taught students to evaluate and run original research on human behavior. Each student picked a question about life in Qatar, wrote a proposal, completed human subjects training, went through IRB review, wrote and piloted a survey, and collected their own data. We covered measurement, scales, sampling, survey design, and experiments along the way, then spent the last weeks on analysis so that every final paper tested its main hypothesis with regression. The semester ended with three days of student research presentations.

Major assignments

  • Final research report15%

    A 2,000 to 2,500 word paper with introduction, literature review of at least three research articles, hypotheses, methods, results, and the survey as an appendix. Every paper had to test its main hypothesis with regression.

  • Final presentation15%

    A short talk on the study and its results, over three days at the end of the semester.

  • Pilot studies and drafts10%

    CITI human subjects training, two proposal drafts, IRB forms, and a survey draft that classmates took and critiqued in class before the final version went out.

  • Two midterm exams40%

    20% each, on research design and measurement, then sampling, surveys, and experiments.

  • Online quizzes10%

    Eight quizzes on the Babbie readings, lowest score dropped.

  • Participation10%

    Attendance by clicker, and in-class design exercises.

Student work

Every project, by term.

Spring 2013: student survey studies 22 projects
  • Student satisfaction in Education City versus Qatar University. Compared satisfaction at the private branch campuses and the national public university. Survey
  • School environment and the desire to study. Whether the school environment relates to students' motivation to keep studying. Survey
  • Employment expectations of CMU Qatar students. Whether nationality, family, and gender shape what students expect after graduation. Survey
  • First-year friendships. Whether first-year students befriend people who are academically similar or similar in other ways. Survey
  • Pedestrian safety in Qatar. Whether pedestrians feel safe on Doha's roads, and what urban planners could change. Survey
  • Mass media and body image. Effects of mass media on body image, eating habits, and exercise. Survey
  • Attitudes toward weight-loss surgery. Whether people accept surgical methods of losing weight over diet and exercise. Survey
  • Creativity. What relates to self-reported creativity among students. Survey
  • Online piracy by students in Qatar. Whether money and the lack of paid alternatives predict how much students pirate. Survey
  • Extracurricular activities and grades. Whether taking part in extracurricular activities affects academic performance. Survey
  • Eating habits on and off campus. Whether students living off campus eat more healthily than those in the dorms. Survey
  • Social capital and academic achievement. Whether relationships with friends and professors relate to grades. Survey
  • Music and aggression. Whether taste in music relates to aggressive attitudes. Survey
  • Buying a car in Qatar. Consumer views of the car market and dealers. Survey
  • Parental control and childhood obesity. Whether parents' control over eating and activity relates to obesity, given high overweight rates in Qatar. Survey
  • Motivation and academic performance. How student motivation relates to grades. Survey
  • Sports in university life. What keeps students from taking part in campus sports. Survey
  • Mobile phone use and academic productivity. Whether heavier phone use goes with lower academic performance. Survey
  • Social media in Qatar. How people in Qatar use social media. Survey
  • Internet use and happiness. Whether how often people use the internet relates to depression and happiness. Survey
  • Why people lie. Whether people with different characteristics tell different kinds of lies. Survey
  • Gender and computer literacy in Qatar. Whether men and women in Qatar differ in computer literacy. Survey

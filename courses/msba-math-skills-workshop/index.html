No. 03

Method

How the course works

Each module ends where an analytics course picks up. Calculus ends with gradient descent and the sigmoid. Linear algebra ends with the normal equations, PCA, and PageRank. Probability works through a fraud alert that is 99% sensitive and still right only 9% of the time at a 0.5% base rate.

I wrote the scope and sequence with the MS in Business Analytics core faculty, working backward from what their courses need. Before recording, I checked every equation symbolically.

No. 04

What students hand in

Major assignments

Pause-and-try problems

Every lesson stops at least once for a problem set in the running case, such as a new break-even point for the coffee shop or the standard error for a smaller hospital sample. Students pause, work it on paper, then watch the solution worked out by hand.

Module 4 hands-on practice

A full lesson of five exercises on one week of Steel City Streaming data: center, spread, a histogram, a box plot, and a correlation, ending with a one-paragraph analyst's report for an executive.

Diagnostic pretest, module quizzes, and posttest

The workshop is designed to open with a diagnostic pretest that shows each student which topics need the most work, give a short low-stakes quiz with immediate feedback at the end of each module, and close with a comprehensive posttest.

No. 05

Revision history

How the course developed

A self-paced online course for incoming MS in Business Analytics students, built with Tepper's learning technologies team between December 2025 and August 2026. I recorded every lesson, about thirty short videos across six modules, each pairing slides with worked problems written out by hand.

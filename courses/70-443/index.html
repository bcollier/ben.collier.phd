70-443 Digital Marketing and Social Media Strategy

70-443

Search, social, and the digital marketing mix, taught through cases and real clients.

19 students in 2014 and 14 in 2015

This was the first time the course was taught at CMU Qatar, and about half the students came from Information Systems and half from Business Administration. In 2014 students spent Thursdays working for real clients, from the university's own marketing office to a board game store in Pittsburgh, doing search engine optimization, social media campaigns, and user-generated content. In 2015 I rebuilt it as a case course in four modules, from the digital landscape through advertising and promotion, the other three P's, and how traditional firms make the move to digital. Students also ran a search and analytics audit for a small Doha business with Google Analytics and AdWords.

Major assignments

  • Search and analytics audit

    In groups of two or three, students worked with the website of a small Doha dog-services business using Google Analytics, Webmaster Tools, and AdWords: understand the client's objectives and keywords, analyze traffic, propose search improvements, and plan a paid search campaign, in an 8 to 12 page report.

  • Air France search campaign analysis

    Using the Air France Internet Marketing case and its campaign data, groups identified the metrics that matter, judged which search publishers to fund or drop, and recommended keywords and an overall digital mix.

  • Case write-ups40%

    A reflection paper at the end of each of the four modules, two individual and two in groups.

  • Participation and quizzes40%

    Case discussion is the course, so participation was 30%, with short in-class and online quizzes for the other 10%.

  • Final exam20%

    A digital marketing case handed out 48 hours ahead, with analysis questions answered in the exam.

  • Real-world client project (2014)

    In 2014, groups worked all semester for a real organization, presenting three times: a search engine marketing plan, a social media campaign, and a user-generated content idea, graded on understanding of the client and quality of analysis.

Student work

Every project, by term.

Spring 2014: client projects 5 projects
  • CMU Qatar Marketing and Public Relations. Audited the university website's metadata, alt tags, and inbound links against other Education City campuses, proposed English and Arabic keywords for the Computer Science and Information Systems programs with cost-per-click estimates, and planned social media around orientation, events, and an alumni blog. Search engine optimization, sponsored search, social media
  • A board game store in Pittsburgh. Set up Instagram and Twitter accounts, linked them to the store's Facebook page, and promoted its Tabletop Day event with hashtags and game quizzes. The owner reported about 200 visitors that day and sales four times the previous year's event. The team proposed a photo contest with the café across the street. Website review, social media campaign, user-generated content
  • An Arabic-learning games startup for young children. Compared the site's domain authority with two competitors, rewrote its title and meta description, recommended AdWords keywords with a budget, and suggested inbound links from Doha schools. Competitor analysis, SEO, AdWords, social media
  • A children's nursery in Doha. Fixed spelling and on-page content, added meta tags, got the site indexed by Google, and built the social strategy around the nursery's inactive Facebook page. SEO and social media
  • An architecture and design consultancy. Added meta tags so the firm appeared in searches for its own name, studied competitors' keywords, and found design and architecture sites to link to it, aiming at Qatar, Amsterdam, and Lebanon. On-page and off-page SEO, social media, user-generated content

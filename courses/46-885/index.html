No. 03

Method

How the course works

Students use the same labs, weekly data stories, and AI coach as the MBA sections. The last module covers explainable AI and visualization for machine learning, so the course ends where their machine learning courses begin.

No. 04

What students hand in

Major assignments

Final visualization project 30%

Individually or in pairs, students answer their own business question or take one of two set datasets (daily city temperatures or the Netflix catalog). They hand in a Tableau workbook with an interactive dashboard, the cleaned data, and a 5 to 7 minute recorded video that tells the story. A one to two page proposal comes due in the last module.

Data story analysis 10%

Once in the term, a small team opens class with a data story published in the last six months, critiques two or three of its charts with principles from the course, and presents remade versions. Before presenting, teams test the redesign with the AI Data Visualization Coach.

Class participation 20%

Credit in each of the seven modules for in-class quizzes, Tableau workbooks submitted from the lab sessions, or follow-along activities.

Labs 40% four labs

Lab 1

Design of visual information

No software yet. Students critique and redesign weak charts, including two published charts claiming college no longer pays off, then critique and redo a chart they made themselves before the course.

Lab 2

Data visualization with Tableau

With a DonorsChoose extract where each row is a donation, students sort measures from dimensions, build a dual-axis chart of donations by grade level, write a students-reached-per-dollar calculated field, map donations by state, add a moving average, and report one insight of their own.

Lab 3

Calculations in Tableau

Using avocado sales data, students build table calculations such as percent of total by market and FIXED and EXCLUDE level of detail expressions, and explain how filters interact with them.

Lab 4

Visualizing spatial data

Did Austin's 2015 Uber ban hurt demand for Airbnb listings that are harder to reach? Students map transit and walk scores, map the change in demand from 2015 to 2016, and cluster listings by location in Tableau.

Revision history

How the course developed

I first taught it in Spring 2025 and again in Spring 2026, with one session a week. It follows the MBA online hybrid syllabus module for module, with typography and color folded into the design sessions, and ends with the same recorded data story.

Executive education

Business Negotiation and Conflict Resolution

Three mornings of live negotiation simulations, each followed by a debrief and a short lecture

91 participants from 15 organizations in the November 2014 run

I co-taught this course with philosopher David Emmanuel Gray four times between 2014 and 2016. Each morning starts with a negotiation that participants play against each other in randomly assigned pairs or groups, then we debrief the actual results before any theory. The arc runs from a two-party job offer, to a property deal where the money alone has no zone of agreement, to a six-party commodity auction where coalitions decide who wins. Participants leave with BATNA, ZOPA, reservation and target prices, and a plan for dealing with hard bargainers.

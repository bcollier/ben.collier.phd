A dated log of teaching, advising, and practice.

  1. August 25, 2026

    Taught the first class of 70-445 Artificial Intelligence for Business Leaders, a new undergraduate course I built. Students learn how AI works, work hands-on with AI agents, and then judge where AI creates value across marketing, finance, operations, and strategy, and how to defend that judgment to executives.

  2. August 24, 2026

    Started the third run of 45-884 AI Methods for Social and Visual Data, with agents moved earlier in the term and a summary and cleaned transcript for students after every class.

  3. August 2026

    Finished recording the MSBA Math Skills Workshop, a self-paced course for incoming MSBA students that runs from algebra through gradient descent, PCA, and statistical inference.

  4. June 10, 2026

    George Leland Bach Teaching Award, voted by the MBA Class of 2026.

  5. June 2026

    Really proud of this summer's AI Methods final projects. Three of the nineteen are already in use, and they range from social listening on an aircraft maker's safety crisis to an offline tool that helps bomb-disposal teams identify ordnance.

  6. May 4, 2026

    Taught AI Methods for Social and Visual Data for the second time, rebuilt for the summer with one live session a week and hands-on Python videos for every module.

  7. April 30, 2026

    Talked with Tepper for a Faculty Spotlight on what students learn in the MSBA, and why I describe business analytics as a decathlon. Watch the video

  8. March 11, 2026

    Started teaching 46-887 Machine Learning for Business Applications to the in-person MSBA cohort, redesigned around AWS. Students take a model from S3 through SageMaker to a database they run themselves and a live dashboard.

  9. January 13, 2026

    Started teaching 90-803 Machine Learning Foundations with Python at Heinz College, rebuilt around four applied projects on policy questions, from broadband subsidies to student loan complaints.

  10. January 2026

    Advising two MSBA capstone teams this spring.

  11. October 22, 2025

    Taught 70-377 Managing and Assessing Tech Talent and Organizations for CMU Qatar, my first course in Doha since 2016: mock technical interviews on Zoom, then an in-person week on teams and culture.

  12. October 2025

    Taught Data Mining in person and online hybrid in the same mini, and added principal component analysis to the course.

  13. August 26, 2025

    Taught the first class of 45-884 AI Methods for Social and Visual Data, a course I built for MBA and master's students on text, images, and AI agents, using foundation models rather than training them.

  14. May 2025

  15. May 2025

    Proud of this spring's Data Mining final projects: teams brought their own questions, from predicting baseball Hall of Fame induction to siting EV chargers in Pennsylvania.

  16. March 2025

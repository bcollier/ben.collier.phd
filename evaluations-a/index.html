The teaching signature

Recent results are strongest where the comments are strongest: respect, relevance, explanation, and care.

4.90

Respect is the clearest through-line.

Across 509 responses since 2025, “shows respect for all students” is the highest-rated dimension. Comments repeatedly add patience, approachability, and willingness to revisit difficult material.

What students notice

A keyword-assisted coding pass across 370 anonymous comments surfaces the recurring ideas. A comment may contribute to more than one theme.

What already lands

Engagement (105 mentions), applied learning (99), and care/accessibility (86) appear most often. Students value a practical classroom with visible enthusiasm and human support.

What to keep tuning

Pace/workload (49 mentions), organization (38), and feedback/grading (29) recur as improvement themes, especially in compressed technical courses. These are mention counts, not satisfaction scores.

Constructive ideas to keep improving

Students’ most useful suggestions are specific and actionable. These exact comments were selected because they explain what could change while remaining respectful and solution-oriented.

AI-assisted analysis and authorship

This report was prepared with GPT-5.6 Sol (High reasoning) in the Academic edition with FERPA-enabled features. The model worked from de-identified course-evaluation data.

What the model did

Supported comment extraction and normalization, keyword-assisted thematic coding, grouping into strengths, neutral observations, and improvement areas, candidate quote selection, and drafting the report narrative and page language.

What was calculated directly

Ratings, response counts, response-weighted averages, and time trends were calculated directly from the source records. The model did not generate or estimate missing ratings.

Quote and privacy safeguards

Displayed student quotations retain the supplied source wording rather than being rewritten by AI. Student identifiers were excluded, and colleague names and individual colleague ratings were not included in the report dataset.

Public verification note

The displayed totals and response-weighted averages were recalculated from de-identified section results before publication. The public page intentionally excludes institutional comparison fields and private source-document hashes. The private audit remains available to the author.

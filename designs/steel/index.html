01 — About

Hot metal, cooled into something you can build with.

Pittsburgh made steel by pouring something molten into a shape that held. That is roughly what I do with data, and it is where Hot Metal AI got its name: the raw material is hot, unstructured, and useless until somebody gives it a form a decision can stand on.

My appointment is at the Tepper School of Business, teaching across the MBA, the MS in Business Analytics, and undergraduate programs, with selected courses at Heinz College. The PhD is from Carnegie Mellon in Information Systems and Organizational Behavior. I came to machine learning through how organizations behave, which is why my labs care as much about whether a finding should change anyone's actions as about whether the model converged.

Before returning to the faculty I led data science at Duolingo through the IPO and the launch of Duolingo Max, and ran data science at UPMC as founding data scientist on a joint venture with IBM Watson Health, leading a drug price forecasting product later acquired by Premier. Four years before that I taught organizational behavior and co-directed executive education at Carnegie Mellon Qatar.

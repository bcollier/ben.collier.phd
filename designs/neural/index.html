01 / About

Models are instruments, not decisions.

My appointment is at Tepper, where I teach across the MBA, the MS in Business Analytics, and undergraduate programs, plus selected courses at Heinz College. I hold a PhD in Information Systems and Organizational Behavior from Carnegie Mellon, which means I came to machine learning through the question of how organizations actually behave, not the other way round.

Before returning to the faculty I led data science at Duolingo through the IPO and the launch of Duolingo Max, and before that ran data science at UPMC as founding data scientist on a joint venture with IBM Watson Health. That work is still going, now through Hot Metal AI and gAIm Systems. It is what keeps the labs from drifting into toy problems.

What I care about in a classroom is the moment a student stops asking whether the model converged and starts asking whether anyone should act on it.

Teaching

My appointment is at Tepper, across the MBA, the MS in Business Analytics, and undergraduate programs, plus selected courses at Heinz College. The PhD is from Carnegie Mellon in Information Systems and Organizational Behavior, so I arrived at machine learning through the question of how organizations behave rather than the other way round. That order is why my labs spend as much time on whether a finding should change anyone's actions as on whether the model converged.

In 2026 the graduating MBA class voted me the George Leland Bach Teaching Award.
